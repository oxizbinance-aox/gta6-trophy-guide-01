    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours, in a single playthrough. There are no online trophies, no mission gold medals and no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off your platinum save.

    Missables — only one trophy can be lost

    Every other trophy, and everything on the 100% Completion checklist, stays available in free roam after the story. The one exception is below. The first point of no return is the Chapter 1 mission Tourist Trap — starting it costs you your pocket cash and the Key Lento house (money in the Family Savings account is kept).

    Everything else is safe

    Story trophies pop as you play and cannot be missed. Stranger quest givers can go temporarily unavailable mid-story (Kenton is the one guides name), but every trophy-related character is back in free roam after the ending. Optional missions with black icons and pink letters do disappear after a while — that costs you nothing toward trophies or 100%.
